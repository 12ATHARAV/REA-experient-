Welcome to

Bartan Time — Kitchen-Chore Nostalgia, Rebuilt in Sound

Bartan Time (बर्तन टाइम) is a free ambient radio built around one cozy, familiar feeling — golden-hour light through the kitchen window, hands in warm soapy water, and a song humming along in the background while the dishes pile down. It's part of the Deluxe Saloon family of nostalgic Indian ambient radios at deluxsalon.in. Press play, and let the sink-side playlist and the little dialogue card carry you through the chore.

FAQ

Bartan Time, Explained

What is Bartan Time?

Bartan Time (बर्तन टाइम) is a free ambient radio at deluxsalon.in built around the cozy, golden-hour nostalgia of doing the dishes at home — sink-side dialogues, soft bubbly visuals, and a nonstop nostalgic playlist.

Is Bartan Time free to use?

Yes. Bartan Time streams entirely for free in your browser, with no sign-up required, on both desktop and mobile.

What kind of music plays on Bartan Time?

Bartan Time plays a curated nostalgic playlist in shuffle — cozy, familiar songs, the kind you'd hum along to while washing up. The same playlist is also available on YouTube, linked in the footer.

Where is the Bartan Time playlist website?

deluxsalon.in/bartantime is the official Bartan Time website — the home of the full bartan playlist and the Bartan Time radio experience.
