Bus Driver — The Highway Raat Playlist

Bus Driver (also searched as bus driver playlist, bus driver website, bus driver playlist website, bus driver music, truck wala or truck driver playlist) is a free ambient radio at deluxsalon.in/busdriver built for one very specific feeling: a decorated Indian truck or state bus rolling down NH48 well past midnight, dashboard lights glowing, "Horn OK Please" painted on the tailgate. Press play and Bus Driver streams a non-stop mix of trucker songs and truck wala song favourites — the kind of bus driver song you'd catch crackling out of a cabin radio on a long highway raat run. Bus Driver plays a non-stop trucker songs mix — truck wala song and bus driver song favourites, the kind of highway raat playlist you'd hear on a decorated truck or bus cabin radio. The same playlist is also available on YouTube, linked in the header above.

What does Horn OK Please mean?

"Horn OK Please" is the classic sign painted on the back of Indian trucks and buses, asking drivers behind to honk before overtaking. Bus Driver turns it into a tap-to-honk button up top, a small nod to that highway culture.

Is deluxsalon.in/busdriver a bus driver website I can trust?

Yes. deluxsalon.in/busdriver is a dedicated bus driver playlist website that streams openly in your browser through YouTube's official embedded player, with no downloads or sign-up needed.
