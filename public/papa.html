Papa Ke Zamane Ke Gaane, Explained

What is Papa Ke Zamane Ke Gaane?

Papa Ke Zamane Ke Gaane (पापा के जमाने के गाने) is a free retro Bollywood radio and playlist — non-stop purane gaane and Kishore Kumar era classics, at deluxsalon.in/papa.

Is it free to use?

Yes — completely free, no sign-up, streams straight from your browser on desktop or mobile.

What's with the chappal button?

A small nod to every Indian dad's favourite disciplinary prop — tap it on the player for a random mock-scolding one-liner, all in good fun.
